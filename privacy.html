Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

The short version: your memories stay on your devices and in your own iCloud. We do not run a server that stores them, we do not sell data, and we do not show ads. The app has no analytics, no accounts and no ad trackers, so we collect no data about you.

Who we are

Stratus ("we", "us") makes the Stratus app for iPhone. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. If you have questions, email hello@getstratusapp.com.

What you put in Stratus

Stratus stores what you choose to save: spoken or typed memories, photos, shared links and notes, and the people, places and plans it recognizes in them. This content is stored:

  • On your device, in the app's private storage.
  • In your private iCloud account, if iCloud is on, so it syncs between your devices. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy. We cannot access it.

How Stratus processes it

  • Voice: speech is transcribed on your device. Audio is not sent to us.
  • Understanding and answers: sorting memories and answering questions use Apple's on-device model. Your memories are not sent to us or to an AI provider.
  • Photos: text in photos is read on your device.
  • Links: when you save a link, the app fetches that page's title and preview image directly from the website, the same way a browser would.

Permissions

Stratus asks for access only when you use a feature that needs it, and you can change any of these in iOS Settings at any time.

  • Microphone and speech recognition: to save memories by voice.
  • Camera and photos: to save photos you take or choose.
  • Contacts: to match people you mention with the right contact and spelling. Contacts are read on your device and are not uploaded.
  • Calendar and Reminders: to add the events and reminders you ask for, and to check that they still exist.

Services you connect

If you connect Todoist or Google Calendar, Stratus sends those services only the tasks or events you ask it to create, directly from your device. Sign-in tokens are stored in your device's Keychain. Your use of those services is covered by their own privacy policies. You can disconnect them in Settings at any time.

Google user data

This section explains how Stratus accesses, uses, stores, shares, retains and deletes Google user data when you choose to connect Google Calendar. Connecting Google Calendar is optional.

Data Stratus accesses

Stratus requests one Google permission, the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events scope ("View and edit events on all your calendars"). With it, Stratus accesses only:

  • The events Stratus creates for you: their title, notes, date and time, and Google's event identifier.
  • Your primary calendar's time zone, so event times are correct.

Stratus does not read, list or search your other calendar events, and it does not access your Google profile, email, contacts or any other Google data.

How Stratus uses it

  • To create a calendar event when you say or type an event into Stratus and choose to send it to Google Calendar.
  • To update or delete that same event when you edit or delete it in Stratus.
  • To read back that same event, for example to check that it still exists.

Google user data is used only to provide these user-facing calendar features. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not used to build user profiles, and not used to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

How it is stored and protected

Requests go directly from your iPhone to Google over encrypted HTTPS. Stratus has no servers, so Google user data never reaches us. The Google sign-in token is stored in your device's Keychain. The identifiers and details of events Stratus created are stored on your device with the memory they came from, and in your private iCloud account if you turn on iCloud sync, where Apple encrypts it and we cannot access it.

Sharing

Stratus does not share, transfer or disclose Google user data to anyone. No person at Stratus can read it.

Retention and deletion

Event details are kept on your device only as long as the memory they belong to. Deleting the memory in Stratus deletes the event from Google Calendar and removes its details from your device after Recently Deleted is emptied (at most 30 days). Disconnecting Google Calendar in Stratus Settings deletes the sign-in token from your device. You can also revoke Stratus's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. To ask a question about your data, email hello@getstratusapp.com.

Limited Use

Stratus's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Purchases

Stratus is a one-time purchase through the App Store. Payment is handled by Apple, and we never see your payment details.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your personal information.
  • We do not use your memories for advertising or to train AI models.
  • We do not include analytics or advertising trackers in the app, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Diagnostics

If you have opted in to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with us. These do not include your memories.

Deleting your information

Deleted memories go to Recently Deleted and are permanently removed after 30 days, or sooner if you empty it. Deleting the app removes its data from your device. To remove synced data, delete it from iCloud in iOS Settings. You can export everything first from Settings in the app.

Children

Stratus is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
